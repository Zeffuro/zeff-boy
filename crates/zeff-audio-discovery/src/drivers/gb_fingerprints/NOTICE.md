# GB fingerprint-source attribution

The signature bytes and ordered candidate rules in `patterns.rs` were
independently transcribed as data from
[`bbbbbr/gbtoolsid`](https://github.com/bbbbbr/gbtoolsid/tree/5ff49ad1282178eaebf47314d0c775c2b13d98b8),
files `entry_names_music.h` and `sig_music.c`, pinned at
`5ff49ad1282178eaebf47314d0c775c2b13d98b8`. That project is released under
the [Unlicense](https://unlicense.org). No upstream scanning implementation is
included. These are family candidates only.
